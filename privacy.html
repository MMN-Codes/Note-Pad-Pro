Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: JULY 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Note Pad Pro. Your privacy is important to us. The app follows an offline-first approach — your notes are stored locally on your device, and no account or sign-up is required to use the core app.

2. Permissions We Use

Note Pad Pro requests permissions only when they are needed for specific features. Depending on the features you use, these may include:

  • Photos / Media: Used to select images from your device when a feature requires them, and to save backup files, PDF documents, or images. Selected images are processed on the device and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Display Over Other Apps: Used by the optional Floating Note feature so you can view or work with a note while using another app.
  • Notifications & Alarms: Used for note reminders and sticky notifications. On supported Android versions, the app may request the Alarms & Reminders permission so reminders can trigger at the exact time you set.
  • Biometric Authentication: Used to lock and unlock the app and, optionally, individual notes using your device's fingerprint, face unlock, or system screen lock. Note Pad Pro does not see, store, or transmit your biometric data — authentication is handled by Android's secure biometric system.
  • Battery Optimization Exemption: May be requested optionally to help reminders work reliably when the app is closed. This is not required unless you choose to use reminders.

3. Data Security & Backups

  • Your notes remain on your device by default. We do not collect or sell your personal notes.
  • Note Pad Pro does not require an account and does not provide cloud synchronization for your notes.
  • The app maintains an automatic local backup on your device as a safety measure against accidental data loss. This local backup is removed when the app is uninstalled.
  • You can also manually create a backup file and save it wherever you choose, including third-party cloud storage such as Google Drive, using your device's file picker.
  • We do not operate, access, or have visibility into third-party cloud storage where you choose to keep your backup files. Those services handle your data according to their own privacy policies.
  • Notes moved to the Recycle Bin are automatically and permanently deleted after 30 days. You can restore a deleted note at any time before permanent deletion.

4. Advertising

Note Pad Pro is supported by advertising through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers, such as the Google Advertising ID, and usage data to provide and measure advertising, including personalized advertising where permitted.

In the European Economic Area and United Kingdom, we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads where required by applicable privacy regulations. You can review or withdraw advertising-related consent from within the app.

For more information about how Google handles data, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Premium Access & Rewards

Certain features may be unlocked temporarily by watching a rewarded video advertisement or by using an invite code shared by another user.

These options do not require payment information. Note Pad Pro does not process or store financial or card details within the app.

6. Children's Privacy

Note Pad Pro is a general-purpose application and is not specifically designed or marketed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can address the matter.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the app, our practices, or applicable laws. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.
